# Brief, voice, and audience

## Load when
Checking objective, audience, funnel stage, intended conversion, tone, specificity, or authenticity.

## Questions answered
Does this exact candidate do the assigned job for the assigned reader in the supplied voice?

## Method
1. Compare the draft's objective, audience, funnel stage, and conversion field by field.
2. Identify the reader knowledge and pain assumed by the language.
3. Check whether specificity, terminology, and stance fit supplied brand context.
4. Reject generic polish that erases a defensible voice or false insider language unsupported by context.
5. Separate voice defects from factual grounding and surface clarity defects.

## Required inputs
Ryan brief, Temi item, brand context, applied constraints, and exact draft.

## Expected contract effect
Brief-alignment and brand-voice checks with relevant constraint references.

## Failure modes
Substituting personal taste, demanding a different strategy, confusing formality with authority, or citing the skill as brand guidance.

## Evidence and authority
Only supplied context and constraints define alignment. Dara cannot redefine audience, strategy, or voice.
