# Grounding and claims

## Load when
The draft states facts, outcomes, comparisons, causal claims, assumptions, or persuasive conclusions.

## Questions answered
What is asserted? What exact supplied evidence supports it? Does the conclusion exceed that support?

## Method
1. Inventory explicit and implied factual claims.
2. Map each claim to supplied moment or angle IDs.
3. Separate source fact, interpretation, assumption, and CTA.
4. Reject stronger scope, causality, certainty, or universality than the evidence permits.
5. Test persuasive reasoning for missing premises, ignored objections, and decorative data.
6. Cite only the evidence relevant to the check or issue.

## Required inputs
Draft claims and assumptions plus supplied moments, angles, and evidence references.

## Expected contract effect
Grounding checks with exact evidence IDs and claim-scoped correction issues.

## Failure modes
Treating plausible language as proof, accepting invented metrics, importing outside knowledge, or demanding evidence for nonfactual style choices.

## Evidence and authority
The method evaluates supplied evidence; it is not evidence and grants no permission.
