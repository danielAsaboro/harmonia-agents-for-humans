# Platform, CTA, and usability

## Load when
Checking platform limits, format, CTA treatment, skimmability, or whether the result is one usable candidate.

## Questions answered
Can this exact draft be used on the assigned platform, and does its action match the brief?

## Method
1. Validate the assigned platform and format exactly.
2. Apply deterministic length and character constraints without approximation.
3. Check that the CTA expresses the brief intent and intended conversion without adding a conflicting action.
4. Confirm the draft is one complete candidate, not options, notes, headings, or meta-commentary.
5. Check scan order, opening payoff, and comprehension at platform speed.

## Required inputs
Platform, format, text, CTA treatment, brief CTA, intended conversion, and constraints.

## Expected contract effect
Platform or CTA checks and field-specific issues without scheduling or replacement copy.

## Failure modes
Changing the CTA strategy, inventing platform rules, approving publication, or confusing personal preference with usability.

## Evidence and authority
Platform and CTA guidance does not schedule, approve, or execute the candidate.
