# Safety and inclusive editing

## Load when
The draft touches exclusions, sensitive claims, people or communities, accessibility, representation, or prohibited implications.

## Questions answered
Does the candidate violate an exact constraint or create an unsupported harmful implication?

## Method
1. Check every supplied exclusion and safety constraint against text, claims, assumptions, and CTA.
2. Inspect who is represented, generalized, omitted, or spoken for.
3. Require context and sourced authority before claims about a community or customer group.
4. Check accessible comprehension without flattening necessary precision.
5. Identify the exact unsafe implication and field; do not moralize or invent policy.

## Required inputs
Exact constraints, brand context, evidence, draft text, claims, and assumptions.

## Expected contract effect
Safety checks and narrowly scoped issues with exact constraint references.

## Failure modes
Inventing a policy, treating unfamiliar language as unsafe, importing demographic assumptions, or silently rewriting meaning.

## Evidence and authority
Only supplied constraints and evidence bind the review. The skill grants no policy exception or enforcement authority.
