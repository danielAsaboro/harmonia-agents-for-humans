# Structure and clarity

## Load when
The point is buried, reasoning overlaps, steps are missing, or the draft reads like a brain dump.

## Questions answered
What is the bottom line? Does every sentence serve it? Are the ideas complete without overlap?

## Method
1. State the draft's thesis in one private sentence.
2. Apply BLUF: the opening should expose the useful point, not the discovery process.
3. Apply MECE: identify duplicated jobs and missing logical steps.
4. Remove mental scaffolding, throat-clearing, circular explanation, and repeated conclusions.
5. Check that transitions preserve one readable line of reasoning.
6. Report the defect and desired reader outcome without writing replacement prose.

## Required inputs
Exact draft text, claims, objective, audience, and format constraints.

## Expected contract effect
Precise clarity issues targeting text, claims, or assumptions.

## Failure modes
Equating brevity with clarity, polishing sentences before fixing logic, or rewriting the post in the instruction.

## Evidence and authority
Structural guidance is not evidence and cannot change the approved message or brief.
