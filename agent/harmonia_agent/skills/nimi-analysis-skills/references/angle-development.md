# Angle development

## Load when
Turning grounded findings into source insights, qualified trends, memes, or learning angles.

## Questions and required inputs
What tension, mechanism, proof, surprise, or useful implication does the evidence reveal?
Requires exact supplied IDs and native grounded-search evidence for current external context.

## Method
1. Start with an exact finding and ask what tension, mechanism, proof, or surprise it reveals.
2. Choose one angle type and its matching evidence kind.
3. Require native grounded-search evidence for trend or meme context.
4. State why the evidence makes the angle defensible without proposing a campaign or post.
5. Reject generic restatements and conclusions stronger than their evidence.

## Contract effect
Distinct analytical angles with exact provenance, assumptions, and confidence.

## Expected output
A complete `Angle` with compatible `angleType` and `evidenceKind`; never strategy or final copy.

## Failure modes and authority
Do not call source language a current trend, invent meme currency, define positioning, or write a CTA.
