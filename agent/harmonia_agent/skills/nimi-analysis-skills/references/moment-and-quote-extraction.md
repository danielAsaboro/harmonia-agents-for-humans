# Moment and quote extraction

## Load when
Selecting transcript moments, exact quotes, hooks, or temporal boundaries.

## Questions and required inputs
Which smallest self-contained passage carries the insight, and what setup preserves its meaning?
Requires typed transcript segments and, for media, the supplied duration.

## Method
1. Find one source-contained idea, event, example, tension, or result.
2. Preserve the shortest exact quote that retains its qualification and meaning.
3. Include every segment needed for setup and payoff.
4. Keep start and end times inside cited segments and media duration.
5. Prefer moments that remain intelligible without invented context.

## Contract effect
Self-contained moments with exact quotes, bounded times, source references, and calibrated confidence.

## Expected output
A complete `Moment` within `SourceAnalysis`; never a draft, caption, CTA, or publication instruction.

## Failure modes and authority
Do not splice distant language into a new claim, paraphrase as a quote, or optimize for a strategy Ryan has not supplied.
