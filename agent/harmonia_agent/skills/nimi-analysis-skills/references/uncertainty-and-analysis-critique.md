# Uncertainty and analysis critique

## Load when
Evidence is sparse, ambiguous, contradictory, externally qualified, or likely to invite overclaiming.

## Questions and required inputs
What premise is missing, what alternative reading survives, and what confidence is justified?
Requires the proposed finding and every evidence ID on which it materially depends.

## Method
1. Restate the conclusion and enumerate every supporting reference.
2. Identify missing premises, conflicting observations, and scope limits.
3. Ask whether a weaker conclusion remains useful and fully supported.
4. State bounded assumptions explicitly and lower confidence.
5. Omit the finding when no defensible version remains.

## Contract effect
Honest assumptions and confidence calibrated to the weakest material dependency.

## Expected output
Bounded assumptions and lower confidence in `SourceAnalysis`, or omission of the unsupported finding.

## Failure modes and authority
Do not hide gaps in polished language, use search popularity as truth, or claim verification beyond supplied metadata.
