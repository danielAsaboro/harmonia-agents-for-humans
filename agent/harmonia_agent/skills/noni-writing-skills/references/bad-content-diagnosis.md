# Bad-Content Diagnosis

Before polishing, identify the force weakening the draft:

- Wrong incentive: optimized for output, keywords, or approval rather than
  reader value and the brief's objective.
- Weak idea: consensus summary with no useful angle or earned perspective.
- Weak execution: buried point, overlap, abstraction, or unsupported leap.
- Wrong fit: format, voice, CTA, or sophistication mismatched to the audience
  and platform.

Repair the highest-level defect first: idea, then structure, then evidence,
then sentences. Do not use stylistic polish to disguise missing substance.
