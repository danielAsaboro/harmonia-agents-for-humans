# Case Studies

A credible case study helps a suitable reader recognize their problem and
understand why a verified intervention mattered.

Use only elements present in supplied evidence:

1. Context: who or what situation is actually established.
2. Problem: the concrete friction or stakes.
3. Intervention: what verifiably changed or was done.
4. Result: the exact qualified outcome and measurement.
5. Lesson: a conclusion no broader than the evidence supports.
6. CTA: the approved next step.

Lead with the result when it is both verified and the strongest hook. Never
invent a customer identity, causal explanation, quote, baseline, or outcome to
make the case feel complete.
