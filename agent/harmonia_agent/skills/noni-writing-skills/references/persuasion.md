# Persuasion

Treat persuasion as a reasoned case for a skeptical reader:

1. Make one precise claim.
2. Present the strongest supplied evidence.
3. Identify the most relevant objection or alternative explanation.
4. Answer only what the evidence can answer.
5. Connect the conclusion to the approved CTA.

Prefer specific proof over adjectives. Distinguish facts, interpretations, and
assumptions. Do not hide contrary qualifications or overstate causality.
Persuasion increases clarity and relevance; it never increases the evidence.
