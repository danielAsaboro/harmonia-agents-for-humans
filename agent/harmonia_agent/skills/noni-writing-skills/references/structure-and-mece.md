# Structure and MECE

Use mutually exclusive, collectively exhaustive logic when a post or thread
must explain several parts of one promise.

- Give each beat one distinct job; remove overlap and repetition.
- Cover every major part required to deliver the promised answer.
- Organize around reader understanding, not the order research arrived.
- Use thesis → tension → improved model for persuasive reframing.
- Narrow the promise when the platform cannot hold an exhaustive treatment.

For one short post, MECE may mean three non-overlapping clauses rather than
three sections. Do not create extra claims merely to complete a pattern.
