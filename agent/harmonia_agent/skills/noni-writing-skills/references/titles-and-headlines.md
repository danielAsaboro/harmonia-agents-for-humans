# Titles and Headlines

For social content, the headline may be the first line or thread title. It must
express the real value of this exact piece to a reader who knows less than the
writer.

Useful moves include leading with the benefit, reversing an expectation,
showing the verified work or result, mining one concrete detail, or stating an
already compelling fact plainly.

Check that the headline is specific, understandable, faithful to the body, and
supported at its implied certainty. Avoid clickbait, generic labels, and a
promise broader than the content delivers.
