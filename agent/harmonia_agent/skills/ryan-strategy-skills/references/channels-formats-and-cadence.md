# Channels, formats, and cadence

## Load when

The request spans channels, needs channel roles or format mix, or asks how often
to publish during the strategy horizon.

## Questions answered

- What strategic job does each requested channel perform?
- Which formats best express the evidence and funnel job?
- What content mix balances proof, perspective, education, and conversion?
- What cadence is a recommendation rather than an external schedule?

## Method

1. Consider only operator-requested channels. Copy operational support exactly
   from the supplied supported-channel catalog.
2. Assign one primary role per channel: discovery, authority, education,
   consideration, conversion support, retention, or advocacy.
3. Match format to evidence shape and audience behavior. Do not mechanically
   shrink the same asset across channels.
4. Allocate mix percentages by objective and evidence availability, totaling
   exactly 100 percent.
5. Recommend a cadence range the strategy can sustain without crossing a known
   quality or capacity limit. Leave dates and publication windows to Temi.
6. Mark unsupported channels as recommendations only; Temi cannot operationalize
   them.

## Required inputs

Requested/supported channels, audience, funnel intent, formats supported by the
context, source evidence, performance observations, horizon, and constraints.

## Expected contract effect

Explicit `channelRoles`, `contentMix`, `cadenceGuidance`, and channel/format
candidates on briefs.

## Failure modes

Channel presence without a role, unsupported channels marked operational,
generic best-time claims, cadence presented as scheduled state, identical
cross-posting, or format choices unsupported by evidence.

## Evidence and authority

Channel facts and operational support cite operator context. Performance-based
cadence or format recommendations require verified performance IDs. Ryan never
creates dates, calendar events, schedules, or publishing instructions.
