# Replanning and calendar critique

Load for revision feedback or a final feasibility pass.

1. Bind feedback to the same approved strategy and current snapshot.
2. Identify the precise collision, capacity, sequencing, or readiness problem.
3. Make the smallest plan-level change that resolves it.
4. Recheck horizon, brief fidelity, evidence, cadence, dependencies, and next
   item selection.
5. State assumptions and lower confidence where the snapshot cannot resolve a
   planning question.

Output is a revised `EditorialPlan`, never a revised strategy. Do not silently
discard operator feedback, invent fresher calendar state, or claim an external
calendar mutation.
